SnapTax Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

Summary: SnapTax stores your receipt data locally on your device. We only send receipt images to Claude AI for processing. We do not sell your data.

1. Information We Collect

SnapTax collects the following information:

  • Receipt images and data: Photos you scan and the extracted information (merchant name, date, amount, category). This data is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework.
  • Mileage entries: Business trip information you manually enter, stored locally on your device.
  • Purchase information: If you subscribe to SnapTax Pro, Apple processes your payment. We receive only confirmation of your subscription status.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Receipt processing: When you scan a receipt, the image is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI API to extract receipt details. This transmission is encrypted and the image is not stored by Anthropic beyond the processing request.
  • Local storage: All your financial data is stored locally on your device and is never uploaded to our servers.
  • App improvement: We may collect anonymous crash reports to improve app stability.

3. Third Party Services

  • Anthropic Claude AI: When you scan a receipt, the receipt image is temporarily transmitted to Anthropic's Claude AI API for text extraction and categorization. Images are processed in real-time and are not stored by Anthropic beyond the immediate processing request. Users are informed and must consent before any data is transmitted. See Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy

4. Data Storage and Security

Your financial data is stored locally on your device using Apple's encrypted Core Data framework. Your Claude API key is stored in Apple's secure Keychain. We do not operate servers that store your personal financial data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except as described in this policy (Claude AI for receipt processing, Apple for payments).

6. Children's Privacy

SnapTax is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You can delete all your data at any time from Settings → Delete All Receipts. Deleting the app removes all locally stored data.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We will notify you of significant changes through the app.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this privacy policy? Contact us at:
support@snaptax.app
